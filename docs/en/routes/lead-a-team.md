# I lead a team

<div class="hk-facts" markdown>
<div markdown>
**For**

Tech leads and engineering managers.
</div>
<div markdown>
**Time**

About 10 minutes to understand, about 2 weeks for a pilot.
</div>
<div markdown>
**You take away**

A pilot your team can judge with evidence, and shared context people and AI can read.
</div>

<div class="hk-steps" markdown>

<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 1. Why the system matters more than the model

<p class="hk-meta">Guide · about 10 minutes of reading</p>

Tools and models change every few months. Rules, context, verification, and the habit of learning from failures can stay. A team that standardizes those first can switch tools without starting over.

[What is Harness Engineering?](../concepts/harness-engineering.md)

</div>

<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 2. Put decisions, sources, and owners in one place

<p class="hk-meta">Company Brain · no code needed</p>

Start with the questions your team answers again and again. For each one, record where the answer lives, why it is trusted, and who keeps it current. That record is context people can rely on and AI can read.

[Company Brain template](../start-here/company-brain-template.md)

</div>

<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 3. Run a pilot

<p class="hk-meta">Real work · about 2 weeks</p>

One repository, one team, the working loop, and a one-page decision record for anything hard to reverse. Agree upfront on what good looks like: fewer repeated review comments, fewer reverted changes, faster onboarding of a new person.

[Agentic SDLC for teams](../start-here/agentic-sdlc-for-teams.md)

</div>

<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 4. Measure adoption without measuring people

<p class="hk-meta">Observatory · team and project level</p>

Look at flow, quality, and AI usage per team or project, never per person. Use the maturity model to agree on where you are today and what the next level asks for.

[Maturity model](../adoption/maturity-model.md) · [Engineering Delivery Observatory](../measure-and-improve/engineering-delivery-observatory.md)

</div>

</div>

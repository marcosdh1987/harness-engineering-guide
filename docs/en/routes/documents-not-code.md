<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 1. The Knowledge Harness in a few minutes

<p class="hk-meta">Guide · about 5 minutes of reading</p>

Knowledge work has the same problem as software: useful context is scattered, decisions are hard to find, and lessons vanish after a conversation. A Knowledge Harness makes sources, provenance, owners, and upkeep explicit. You do not need a terminal.

[Engineering Harness and Knowledge Harness](../understand/engineering-vs-knowledge-harness.md)

</div>

<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 2. One recurring question

<p class="hk-meta">Company Brain · no terminal</p>

Pick one question people keep asking. Write down the authoritative sources, why each one is trusted, and who keeps it current. A shared folder or a wiki is enough to start; a repository is optional.

[Knowledge work](../use/knowledge-work.md) · [Company Brain template](../start-here/company-brain-template.md)

</div>

<div class="hk-step" markdown>

### 3. Use AI with that context

<p class="hk-meta">Real work · no terminal</p>

Give the AI the sources you recorded and ask the question. When an answer is wrong, note what was missing or outdated and fix the source, not only the answer. Next time everyone gets the better answer.

[Company Brain, Role Brain and Second Brain](../understand/company-role-second-brain.md)

</div>

</div>

## Three useful levels

| Level | Best for | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Orientation | Everyone | Problem, ecosystem, working loop, and first destination |
| 2. Practice | Practitioners | Context, rules, skills, tools, workflows, and verification |
| 3. Engineering | Harness Engineers and AI Champions | Adapters, routing, evaluations, attribution, regression, and governance |

You can move between levels. The deeper pages are reference material, not a prerequisite for trying the ideas.

## A simple rule

If you are unsure, start with one real workflow, define what good looks like, verify the outcome, and capture the lesson. The amount of ceremony should match the risk and scope of the task.
